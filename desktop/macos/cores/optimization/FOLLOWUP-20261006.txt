Further core investigation against Mac 0.1.9 (2026-10-06)

No new optimization was adopted. The installed 0.1.9 app/core stays unchanged.
This is research evidence, not a new release or a claim of higher hashrate.

PRL: compared 11 shader configurations at 4096-square/K4096, including
32/64/128 K staging, smaller threadgroups and streamed operand loading.
Best cool-run differences were under 1%; deeper staging could be much slower.
After a 180-second sustained run, repeated four small-tile configurations.
Candidate paired median gains were 0.45%, 0.14%, 0.34%; paired mean gains
0.55%, 1.92%, 2.62%. Exploratory bootstrap intervals all cross zero and ignore
serial correlation. Large individual readings do not establish stable gain.
None of these candidates was promoted through full production validation.

PRL sustained K3-only test: 64x64/BK32/PF0/half operands, same fixed buffers
and input, no pool, GUI, Python preparation or adaptive shape changes.
First three 10-second windows average 3.00 TOPS; last three average 2.05 TOPS
(-31.7%). Final full window 1.96 TOPS. NSProcessInfo thermalState changed
from 0 (nominal) to 1 (fair). AC power throughout; other apps were not closed.
This shows that pool or GUI scheduling is not the sole cause of declining
throughput. Correlation with thermal state does not prove an exclusive cause;
no GPU frequency, power or temperature counters were captured. pmset did not
report a warning. Do not disable thermal protection or promise a cooling gain.

QTC WGSL experiments, same-batch 40 ABBA rounds, following CPU parity and
64 strict-target triplets: 32-bit correction -9.358%; selected correction
-7.286%; square carry simplification -0.141%; three-product Karatsuba -30.481%.
The saved multiply in Karatsuba did not outweigh the added carry/subtraction
work in measured throughput. Retain the current four-product implementation.

QTC direct native Metal experiments: exported trusted baseline vs 64-bit
mulhi and explicit 32-bit mulhi products. All pass 96 GPU reference digest/
equal-target checks (screening, not independent CPU validation). Median wall
rates 21.888 / 18.302 / 18.097 MH/s; paired mean -16.55% / -17.43%.
No native backend was integrated. Existing trusted shader options already
omit redundant runtime bounds/loop checks; there was no new gain to apply.

Raw arrays, sustained windows and statistics: evidence/m3-followup-research.json.
Private scripts, generated shaders, full logs: .local/m-series-next and the
same-named remote test-session directory. QTC/PRL production source trees,
installed app, package, fees, proof/admission gates and mining behavior unchanged.
